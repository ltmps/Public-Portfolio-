# Rocket project logo provenance

These three logos identify distinct vehicle, team and competition associations. They do not indicate employment or endorsement.

- `bronco.png`: the Bronco Launch Vehicle emblem from the April 2023 B1LV Senior Project Symposium Presentation, embedded `ppt/media/image6.png`. Exported at 600 × 600 from 975 × 975 without cropping visible artwork.
- `space-enterprise.png`: the Space Enterprise at Berkeley team mark from the header of [the team's official website](https://www.berkeleyse.org/), checked 19 October 2026. [Original image](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/57ef35e203596e0c0570312b/1503883946714-M52V5ZAO57QYBW2EFDOG/logo-berkeley+blue+-+gold+stars.png). Only transparent canvas was trimmed; visible artwork and aspect ratio are retained. Exported at 233 × 600. The selector labels this as a team logo for the Enterprise E-2 project, not a vehicle-specific emblem.
- `far.png`: Friends of Amateur Rocketry emblem from the Singularity Symposium Presentation, embedded `ppt/media/image15.png`. Exported at its original 320 × 312 size. FAR 51025 is the competition association for the UMBRA Singularity student rocket project; FAR is not presented as an employer. The [official 2023 competition document](https://friendsofamateurrocketry.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/2023-FAR-51025-competition.pdf) corroborates that competition identity.

Only these selected logo exports are included. Source decks, other figures, resumes and private contact information are not included. Exports omit source metadata. No logos were generated, recolored, sliced or stretched.
