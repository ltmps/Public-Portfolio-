# SHERPA mission animations

The 17 v5 videos accompany the seven SHERPA mission pages. They comprise 14 main sequences and three alternate views from the completed ARO 4200 mission-render session. `mission-renders.json` records each MP4's SHA256, byte size, duration, frame count, frame rate, audio presence and extracted poster time. The earlier v2 selection and its manifest remain separate.

All selected files are silent H.264, 1280 x 720, 24 fps. Their hashes match the completed render metadata. Three decoded frames per clip were visually inspected; the source session also recorded full decode checks. Posters are frames extracted from the corresponding videos. Each page supplies a description, a time-ordered text sequence, native manual controls and an MP4 link. Videos load on request and have no automatic or hover playback.

The project CAD supplies spacecraft, arm, tank and tool geometry. Added service ports, customer internals, bay supports, hose routes and selected deployment interfaces remain proposed design details. Fluid states are illustrative lumped assumptions; mechanical animations show configured motion; relay views illustrate pointing and links. Entry effects are qualitative. The disposal scene pairs an illustrative transfer with a two-body lunar deorbit example. The public descriptions retain these boundaries beside the relevant videos.

Only the selected MP4s, extracted posters and this compact media inventory are included. Editable scenes, private source documents, runtime paths, raw caches and the portable source archive are outside the website assets.
