# SHERPA mission animations

The 17 v5 videos accompany the seven SHERPA mission pages. They comprise 14 main sequences and three alternate views from the completed ARO 4200 mission-render session. `mission-renders.json` records each MP4's SHA256, byte size, duration, frame count, frame rate, audio presence and extracted poster time. The earlier v2 selection and its manifest remain separate.

All selected files are silent H.264, 1280 x 720, 24 fps. Their hashes match the completed render metadata. Three decoded frames per clip were visually inspected; the source session also recorded full decode checks. Posters are frames extracted from the corresponding videos. Each page supplies a description, timestamp panels and native manual controls. Selecting a panel seeks the video and updates the current-phase description; playback also keeps the active panel in sync. A seek preserves the visitor's play/pause choice. Without JavaScript, the panels retain their timestamps and full text. Full videos load on request and use manual playback.

The seven mission-group cards use separate six-second, silent 640 x 360 / 12 fps excerpts, totaling under 250 KB. `previews.json` records their sources, start times, sizes and hashes. Each preview source is attached only on hover, keyboard focus or an explicit Play preview action. Touch users have a separate play/pause button beside the mission link. Only one preview plays at a time, and it stops on exit, scrolling out of view, tab hiding or its end. Reduced motion disables automatic hover/focus playback; explicit play remains available. Without JavaScript the cards retain posters and normal navigation links.

Mission groups, timestamp panels and mission navigation use single-row horizontal strips with native scrollbars, side padding, touch swipe, keyboard access and previous/next scroll buttons. Active phases scroll horizontally into view without moving the page vertically.

The project CAD supplies spacecraft, arm, tank and tool geometry. Added service ports, customer internals, bay supports, hose routes and selected deployment interfaces remain proposed design details. Fluid states are illustrative lumped assumptions; mechanical animations show configured motion; relay views illustrate pointing and links. Entry effects are qualitative. The disposal scene pairs an illustrative transfer with a two-body lunar deorbit example. The public descriptions retain these boundaries beside the relevant videos.

Only the selected MP4s, extracted posters and this compact media inventory are included. Editable scenes, private source documents, runtime paths, raw caches and the portable source archive are outside the website assets.
