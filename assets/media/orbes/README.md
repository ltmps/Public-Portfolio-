# ORBES workshop recordings

User-approved ORBES uploads, preserved unchanged at repository root:

- `20230603_131600.mp4`, uploaded by Gavin in `6e72b43b06f4f6075f8af66587a5b6ab9f6cbf09`: 11 seconds, 1080×1920, H.264 with AAC audio. The page references this original directly.
- `gif.gif`, uploaded by Gavin in `18abc8a8f9182c4e05700ff74b15d8ed654a8962`: 400×711, 345 frames. `workbench.mp4` is an H.264/yuv420p CRF 23 derivative with faststart and one padding row for even dimensions; no audio, looping or automatic playback.

`suspended-hardware.jpg` and `workbench.jpg` are stills extracted with FFmpeg at 5 seconds and 3 seconds respectively. Both source uploads remain untouched. Representative frames were visually inspected and both displayed videos fully decoded. Captions describe observed hardware placement/handling only, without person identities, control-performance claims or reaction-wheel attribution.

The original MP4 audio has not been independently listened to or transcribed; captions for any speech remain outstanding. Native controls and explicit still links are available with or without JavaScript. All playback starts manually, including under reduced motion.

`rviz-imu-live.mp4` comes from Gavin's AnyDesk screen recording from Jul 24, 2025 at 15:31 (20.8 s, 1808×964). It is cropped to the remote Ubuntu desktop so the AnyDesk address bar, session ID and license banner are removed, scaled to 1280×716, and encoded as H.264 High/yuv420p CRF 23 at 30 fps with faststart and no audio. `rviz-imu-live.jpg` is its final frame, which shows the full acceleration and angular-velocity traces. The Pi clock in the recording reads Jul 24 15:31, and no CSV log in the ORBES repository covers that session.

The Fig. 4 replay (`assets/js/ir-track.js`) draws the 19:27:05.744 to 19:28:28.304 window of the Nov 14, 2025 IR tracking session, from the `select` button press that launched `OBI_Track` to the power-button emergency stop (both in `phase_two/system.log`), from `phase_two/log_misc.txt` in ltmps/ORBES, written by `phase_two/two_cam.py`. Bearing is the logged `[IR CAM] Yaw` value, `atan2(cx - 320, fx)` of the brightest blob, so it is the beacon's horizontal angle from the IR camera boresight; logged `N/A` frames are marked as no beacon in frame (244 of 777 frames, 69% detection). IMU yaw is the logged `ORIENTATION: yaw`, sampled every 0.2 s. The command trace is the mean of motors 0, 3, 5 and 6 minus the mean of motors 1, 2, 4 and 7 from the logged `[MOTOR_CONTROL_THROTTLES] Received` lines, kept at each change. Gavin's phone recording of this run (`PXL_20251115_032704037.mp4`, Google Photos) starts at 19:27:04 Pacific. The page keeps `20230603_131600.mp4`, which has no recording timestamp, so the replay maps one loop of that 11 s video onto the whole 82.6 s log and the page says their timing is not confirmed. In this run only `/dev/ttyUSB0` of the two ESP32 motor links connected; sends to `/dev/ttyUSB1` failed, so the plotted command is what the controller requested.

`assets/images/orbes/fan-platform-architecture.svg` redraws Gavin's draw.io architecture diagram (`fan-platform-architecture.png`) as vector art with the same layout, correcting "Predfined" to "Predefined", "Neckless" to "Necklace" and "OBI (CV)" to "OpenCV".
