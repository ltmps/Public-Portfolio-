# ORBES workshop recordings

User-approved ORBES uploads, preserved unchanged at repository root:

- `20230603_131600.mp4`, uploaded by Gavin in `6e72b43b06f4f6075f8af66587a5b6ab9f6cbf09`: 11 seconds, 1080×1920, H.264 with AAC audio. The page references this original directly.
- `gif.gif`, uploaded by Gavin in `18abc8a8f9182c4e05700ff74b15d8ed654a8962`: 400×711, 345 frames. `workbench.mp4` is an H.264/yuv420p CRF 23 derivative with faststart and one padding row for even dimensions; no audio, looping or automatic playback.

`suspended-hardware.jpg` and `workbench.jpg` are stills extracted with FFmpeg at 5 seconds and 3 seconds respectively. Both source uploads remain untouched. Representative frames were visually inspected and both displayed videos fully decoded. Captions describe observed hardware placement/handling only, without person identities, control-performance claims or reaction-wheel attribution.

The original MP4 audio has not been independently listened to or transcribed; captions for any speech remain outstanding. Native controls and explicit still links are available with or without JavaScript. All playback starts manually, including under reduced motion.

`rviz-imu-live.mp4` comes from Gavin's AnyDesk screen recording from Jul 24, 2025 at 15:31 (20.8 s, 1808×964), uploaded in this session. It is cropped to the remote Ubuntu desktop so the AnyDesk address bar, session ID and license banner are removed, scaled to 1280×716, and encoded as H.264 High/yuv420p CRF 23 at 30 fps with faststart and no audio. `rviz-imu-live.jpg` is its final frame, which shows the full acceleration and angular-velocity traces. The Pi clock in the recording reads Jul 24 15:31, and no CSV log in the ORBES repository covers that session.
