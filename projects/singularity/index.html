Integration & packing

Detail B · Electronics meet recovery

The avionics bay carried GPS, redundant altimeters, batteries and wire access on a printed sled with bulkhead interfaces.

Launch checklist

  1. Line condition.
  2. Coordinated main-parachute folding.
  3. Pack fit.
  4. Harness connections across payload and avionics bay.
  5. Clearance so cords cannot catch on the payload.

Testing

Detail C · Troubleshoot, then repeat

Test 1 · Altimeters

Fix the chamber, then repeat.

Vacuum-chamber tests hit calibration problems. Troubleshooting and repeat tests checked the altimeters alongside recovery pop tests.

Test 2 · Flight

Recovered intact, with an early deployment.

The main parachute deployed prematurely. Follow-up pop tests investigated retention at the main-parachute and avionics-bay connection.

Test 3 · GPS range

From tracker comparison to range test.

Featherweight led the first comparison; the team later selected Big Red Bee. I ran the six-mile field test.
