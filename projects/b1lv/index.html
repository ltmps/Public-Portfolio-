Hardware preparation

Connect the drawings to the parts on hand.

Organize the piping before assembly.

Within the Propellant Feed Systems team, Gavin contributed to piping organization, cleanliness and assembly preparation. The team reviewed 20 launch-vehicle assembly P&ID drawings, cross-checked the Hardware Utilization List, identified missing parts and organized hardware into 15 P&ID segments.

Prepare a workable cleaning area.

When access to MOCAT was unavailable, the team prepared a controlled cleaning area in the Liquid Rocket Lab. The work covered contamination control, inspection, recleaning and preserving cleaned parts for assembly.

Compare pressurant layouts before committing to one.

Gavin contributed to concept development and a pressurant redesign trade study. Two conceptual layouts were compared for safety, potential leakage reduction, compactness, ergonomics and simplicity. The study recorded qualitative tradeoffs to guide a later design choice.

Keep changes traceable.

Gavin maintained meeting notes and design-change records and managed inventory through the Hardware Utilization List. Shared reporting covered piping, tank fatigue and water-flow testing, alongside co-authorship of the piping failure modes and effects analysis (FMEA).
