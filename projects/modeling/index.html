Three bugs the checks found

  1. RPM unit. The constraint diagram stored a rad/s value as the required wheel RPM without converting it. A unit test reproduced the error at all 500 plotted points.
  2. Acceleration unit. Wheel acceleration already in rad/s² was multiplied by 2π/60 again before plotting.
  3. Tuning objective. The open-loop tuning optimized a two-controller loop, but the final response was evaluated with a single controller.

Recordings show simulated behavior under their stated assumptions, not validated dynamics. The selected PID holds ±0.5° in the nominal case, but only 9 of 27 inertia and friction cases pass, so it is not a hardware-ready controller.
