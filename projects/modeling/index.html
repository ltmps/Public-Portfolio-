SCExample / System Composer

Connect requirements to the parts that fulfill them.

The educational Mars-helicopter example separates operations, functions and physical subsystems. Architecture and allocation views make it possible to follow a requirement through the system and inspect the interfaces between components.

Gavin's 2023 work in the inherited SCExample project included System Composer models, stereotypes and allocation diagrams. Continued enrichment in 2026 adds typed interfaces, requirement traceability and executable mission-state behavior using synthetic events and educational assumptions.

Reaction wheels / Simscape & PID

Follow wheel torque into body motion.

The reaction-wheel study connects a multibody model with attitude-control scripts. Quaternion-based PID work, wheel sizing and log analysis explore how rotor inertia, torque and speed limits affect the commanded response.

Reviewing the model exposed unit conversions, inconsistent inertia assumptions and a mismatch between the tuning objective and the final controller. Those issues make parameter consistency and stress-case behavior central to the next model iteration. The scope here is simulated reaction-wheel dynamics.

MALTO / Earth–Encke

Keep a mission configuration distinct from its trajectory picture.

Saved MALTO mission configurations cover comet, Encke and Psyche studies. They record work with an existing mission-design tool and provide a starting point for examining mission assumptions.

A separate Earth–Encke reconstruction study compares direct and Mars-assisted paths. Spacecraft coordinates are reconstructed from plots, with estimated timing and an assumed out-of-plane coordinate. Earth and Mars SPICE tracks and an Encke Horizons track provide separate reference context.

Reconstruction method

  1. Read the plotted pathRecover spacecraft coordinates from the source plots.
  2. Apply stated assumptionsEstimate timing and specify the out-of-plane coordinate.
  3. Compare in contextDisplay the reconstructed path alongside planetary and comet reference tracks.

This sequence describes a visualization method for reconstructed paths.

SHERPA / NASA RASC-AL concept

Turn a servicing mission into a system concept.

The student team's SHERPA proposal explored a multi-use platform near Earth–Moon L1 for spacecraft servicing, refueling and communications relay. Its concept of operations connects arrival, docking and servicing with architecture choices, technology assumptions, mass estimates and budget planning.

Gavin is a named member of the proposal team. This entry presents the shared mission-concept study and its system-level tradeoffs.

ROS2 / ORBES integration

Exercise the data path before connecting every sensor.

ROS2 simulation work used CSV and IMU replay alongside synthetic camera data to exercise integration. Replayed and generated inputs provide a controlled setting for following data through the pipeline and separating interface behavior from physical sensor behavior.

The related ORBES case study follows the fan platform's hanging tests, frame-alignment investigation and motor-allocation work.
