Can reorientation recover lost authority?

With m12 dead, remaining lateral fans can brake world-frame motion after a pitch maneuver. In the separate 60 s Simscape experiment, a planned 90° turn and return reaches the final position within 0.133 mm and holds upright. The half-flip and full rotation do not sustain recovery. Delaying the same schedule to a 2 s fault announcement fails.
