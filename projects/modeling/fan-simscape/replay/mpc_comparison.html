What is simulated, and what does the comparison mean?

Closed-loop comparisons use the same repository Python MPC in both engines: 10 prediction steps of 0.1 s, 10 Hz control, and the same weights, solver settings and fan limits. Three closed-loop cases pass the engine gate, with worst differences 2.266 mm / 0.0646°. Applied-thrust replay instead sends recorded MuJoCo fan commands into Simscape with its controller bypassed; two of those XML-geometry replays fail the gate.

The replay synchronizes position, attitude, velocity, angular rate and fan forces from the simulation. Fans are modeled as ideal force sources. Motion and force indicators play at 20 Hz; plots and peak values use 100 Hz samples.

The mounted bottom TAG camera looks along body −Z. M/S identify the MuJoCo/Simscape floor-tag projections; the tag can leave the geometric field of view during tumble. The unused IR target is hidden. There is no image noise, occlusion model or vision estimator. Fan holes and bars use the same logged applied thrust; MuJoCo bars are outlined and Simscape bars solid. Path colors identify engines, not success.

Both engines use matching model, controller and physical parameters. The controller receives the simulated state directly under nominal conditions. Fault response, sensor fusion and real-time hardware performance require separate tests.

Why do two applied-input replays fail?

The authoritative Simscape brief rounds XML fan points by up to 40 µm per coordinate (53.85 µm displacement per point). Tiny moment-arm differences accumulate without feedback. The offset replay differs by 7.218 mm; the compound replay by 51.090 mm / 6.392°. These primary failures are shown in the selector.

Separate diagnostic MuJoCo models match the brief fan geometry in memory and reduce the physics step to 0.1 ms. All three then pass against Simscape replay, with worst differences 1.237 mm / 0.0151°. These diagnostic variants isolate geometry and numerical contributions to the discrepancy. The original XML-geometry results remain in the comparison.
