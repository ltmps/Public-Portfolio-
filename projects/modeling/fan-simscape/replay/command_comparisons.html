What is being commanded and compared?

The controller requests the same position and upright attitude in every run. The fault is imposed after its fan commands, or as the selected external force/torque. The healthy trajectory was rerun in the same comparison runner; it matches the earlier healthy GNC 1 position trajectory exactly. No prescribed T1 sinusoidal wrench is mixed into these comparisons.

The bottom TAG camera looks along body −Z, and the forward IR camera along body +X, using the repository mounts. The bottom-camera panel shows healthy and faulty geometric projections of the same floor tag. GNC 1 does not use IR tracking, so its IR target and panel are hidden.

These are simple truth-state position/attitude PD demonstrations on the Simscape plant, not the flight DLQR/EKF or its acceptance suite. The floor at z=0 is a reference plane, without contact. A trajectory below it is a reference violation, not a simulated impact. Original CSVs are 100 Hz; this viewer interpolates a 20 Hz display copy. All panels use the same simulation time.
