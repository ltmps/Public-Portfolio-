What was checked and assumed?

Fourteen bounded fans drive the physical articulated model. This animation replays its recorded joints; camera images are re-rendered from those states. The video slows at capture and release, with the true simulation clock shown below.

Continuous twelve-range association keeps the remembered dock pose current without refreshing the camera timestamp. Bounded integral compensation counters steady contact loads without changing fan limits. Once fresh tactile switches confirm seating, the controller holds normal force and damps body rate rather than chasing pose updates. Near contact, a causal 100 ms window of central rear ranges estimates closing speed; IMU samples remain logged. Retention switches sustain seating preload while the physical latch confirms; stronger magnetic holding then supports charging. A smaller backplate, outward-routed collar supports and supported magnetic pads outside the collar give the adaptor insertion clearance; wider supported magnetic landing faces keep attraction normal to the charging surface at small offsets; all collisions remain enabled. Charging requires a fresh latch and both electrical poles at every 1 ms update. Opens receive zero current and energy; the fans stay off while the connector recovers. The cycle requires three accumulated seconds of enabled, connected current above 10 mA, rather than three elapsed seconds. Latch loss aborts; connection recovery is bounded to 5 s and the charging window to 120 s. A separate station release servo uses four ideal root encoders, a 0.26 rad opening reference and a 2.5 mNm torque cap. Departure waits for 0.5 s of settled opening and more than 2 mm hook clearance, which remains checked throughout collar withdrawal. Rear ranges hold the fingers open until the vehicle clears the adaptor. This is an explicit station-actuator upgrade; the original vehicle fan limits and finger springs remain unchanged.

Central rear ranges use ideal MuJoCo rangefinder readings; the twelve-beam pose fit adds 1 mm range noise. The 100 Hz ranging capability, IMU noise, spring material, magnetic force and charging electronics are simulation assumptions, not qualified hardware. The added frame and pole supports contribute 57.978 g under an assumed density; the original 2.7 kg nominal controller mass is unchanged. Pose uncertainty remains uncalibrated. Contact-servo commands and bounded unpowered release waits are separately audited; neither is an MPC safety certificate. This is one finite docking-only cycle, not a completed inspection mission.
