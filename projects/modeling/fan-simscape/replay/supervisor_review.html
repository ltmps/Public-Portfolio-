Simulation, fault timing and camera details

The schematic replay shows position, orientation and fan thrusts from the Simscape Multibody simulation.

Fault notification supplies the failed fan identity to the controller at a scheduled time. The replay clock starts at that notification.

The bottom TAG view uses the actual mounted camera geometry (body −Z); B/R identify baseline/retimed visibility. There is no image noise, occlusion model or estimator. The unused IR target is hidden. The thrust legend is inside the animation; fan holes and bars use the same actual force sample. Baseline bars are outlined; retimed bars are solid. m12 stays off in both.

The retimed candidates came from 72 schedule predictions with delay inside feedback, followed by Simscape Multibody checks. Mode 6 at a 2 s notification changes turn/dwell from 3.5/8 s to 6/40 s. Its worst final-two-second position error improves from 49.656 to 24.004 mm. Mode 4 at 2 s changes dwell from 20 to 40 s. Mode 4 at 0.5 s keeps its 6/40 s roll schedule as a same-schedule control.

All six displayed runs recover under the source criterion: final-two-second position <50 mm, speed <0.02 m/s, upright error <2°, rate <0.02 rad/s, COM height ≥0.15 m, and a 1 rad/s ceiling after the first 5 s. The stricter ceiling applies throughout, including the initial state. No displayed run meets that strict criterion. Rate overshoot after entry is not excused by the separate initial-entry diagnostic.

Both schedules use matching gains, plant and fault geometry. The controller uses a scheduled fault notification and direct simulated-state feedback; ground contact is outside the model. Pose and fan logs are 100 Hz, with motion sampled at 20 Hz for playback. Plots and metrics use the full 100 Hz record.
