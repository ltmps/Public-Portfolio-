Motion and camera details

Pose reach uses a 5 mm / 0.5° screening tolerance, sampled at approximately 10 Hz. Camera availability is measured on reached poses. Clearances use simplified collision geometry; fan cones are geometric keep-out checks, not airflow predictions.

What the comparison supports

MountFinding in this placement
Table cornerReaches the later hold and IR-tracking poses. The first 1.4 s and 0.3 s of acquisition remain outside the tested envelope; the larger motions are more limited.
Fixed wall / overheadMount position and orientation matter. A fixed base cannot cover the full 4 m translation or the full toss excursion.
Overhead X/Y + yawReaches all sampled poses in modes 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 without sampled contacts or fan-cone hits. Mode 5 remains limited.

All five layouts use the KUKA LBR iiwa 14 model, a 2.7 kg ORBES vehicle and a provisional 0.35 kg sensor/bracket assembly. Open-source arm model.

Proposed fixtures include the rigid support, a six-axis force/torque sensor and offset bracket, robot cable routing, and optional translation/yaw stages. ORBES keeps battery power and storage onboard, with wireless telemetry proposed. No external vehicle data/power tether or slack arrest harness is modeled.

Weight support is only part of zero-G emulation. Arm inertia, controller delay and the physical IMU response also matter. Exact-model dynamic tests remain optimistic; the imposed delay stress fails, and raw accelerometer measurements need a separate labeled virtual/HIL channel. The table candidate has not received that dynamic test.

This is an initial layout screen, not hardware qualification or an exhaustive search over mounting positions. The current fan model uses 2.7 kg; the earlier PDR trace lists 3.13 kg. No electronics mass was silently removed.
