Heater-loss recovery

Detail E · Restored vs persistent loss

Heater authority comes back at 65 s in one case and stays lost in the other. The thermal response flips from cooling to heating only when power is delivered.

How it was prepared

Detail G · Six steps from catalog to replay
  1. 01 · PRESERVE ARCHITECTURECarry the 57-element base catalog forward with all 224 relations and 75 flows, so every new behavior traces back to the original architecture.
  2. 02 · DEFINE SCENARIOFixed 1.785 kg mass, 3.71 m/s² Mars gravity, a 3 m cruise target and an obstacle from x = 6 to 10 m. Energy starts at 45 kJ with a 9 kJ reserve.
  3. 03 · LINK BEHAVIORA System Composer component links to Simulink. A sampled controller sets thrust and pitch moment with a 0.2 s lag and ±20° pitch limit.
  4. 04 · EXECUTEThe controller samples every 0.02 s and Aerospace 6DOF integrates at 0.01 s, keeping 5,501 samples per case.
  5. 05 · CHECK INDEPENDENTLYRecompute force balance, energy and thermal closure, clearance, landing gates and telemetry conservation across 47 acceptance and regression checks.
  6. 06 · PRESENT RESULTSReplay the retained traces in MATLAB and export the animations. The browser plays a precomputed export and does not run MATLAB.
