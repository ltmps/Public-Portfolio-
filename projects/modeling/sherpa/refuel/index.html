Hose retrieval, refueling and stow

The arm retrieves the RAFTI tool, an assisting arm opens the customer hatch, and the hose couples to a proposed service port. An isolated helium leak check precedes a 20 kg isothermal top-up, followed by purge, tool withdrawal, hatch closure and stow. The 3 m hose, reel and service circuit are proposed interfaces. Time is compressed; the tank bars illustrate the assumed transfer state.
