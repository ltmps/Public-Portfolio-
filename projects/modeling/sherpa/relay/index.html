Optical relay and terminal pointing

The terminals alternate between customer uplink, Earth downlink and lunar links. Close views show conceptual lander, habitat and VIPER-inspired rover receivers, then azimuth and elevation sweeps and an orbiter relay. Native terminal housings and swivels carry representative mirrors, baffles and receiver apertures. The pointing ranges and compressed link geometry illustrate operations; terminal performance and hardware limits remain design assumptions.
