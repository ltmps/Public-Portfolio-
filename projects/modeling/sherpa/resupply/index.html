YAC docking and resupply

YAC approaches SHERPA and docks at its side-mounted interface. Cutaways show an isolated gas check, liquid transfer with helium pressurization, purge to recovery and dry-break closure before departure. The native vehicle layout is retained with a proposed 350 mm clearance neck and service connections. Colors identify liquid, helium, purge and isolated states.

YAC fluid and gas cutaway

The cutaway follows the YAC and SHERPA tanks through line isolation, pressure hold, transfer and enclosed purge. Cyan liquid and amber helium tracers show the proposed flow paths; purple identifies purge recovery. Tank labels track mass, pressure, temperature and ullage. Enlarged tracking symbols make the flow visible while native propulsion pipes remain CAD context for the assumed service tie-ins.

YAC entry and recovery

The radio antenna stows, the inflatable entry shield deploys, and YAC proceeds through an atmospheric-entry illustration. A parachute and airbags complete the recovery sequence. The native YAC and shield geometry are paired with representative textiles, plasma, heating appearance and timing. These effects visualize the recovery concept rather than calculate entry loads.
