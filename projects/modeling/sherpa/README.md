## Fig. 4B trajectory replay

`assets/js/sherpa-trajectory.js` replays the team's Fig. 4 trajectory plot in an Earth-centered frame, where the Moon and L1 turn around Earth, in the rotating frame of the original plot, or in a tilted 3D view of the Earth-centered frame that readers can turn by dragging or with the arrow keys. The 3D path stays in the Earth-Moon plane, because the original plot is planar. The path is a reconstruction. The departure leg was traced from the plot pixels, and the rest was fitted to the plotted lines as a chain of short circular restricted three-body arcs (Earth-Moon mass ratio 0.01215), so the timing is approximate and some loops of the original are not retraced. The small orbit around L1 follows the drawn ellipse and continues for one full lunar orbit after arrival, so L1 makes one complete turn around Earth in the Earth-centered views. A SHERPA tag follows the spacecraft. Each legend entry is a toggle button that hides or shows its markers and labels, and when L1 passes the Burn 2 marker the L1 and Moon labels move to the side away from it. The page keeps the original figure, and the replay markup holds a static rotating-frame path for readers without JavaScript. Reduced motion shows the whole path and waits for Play.
