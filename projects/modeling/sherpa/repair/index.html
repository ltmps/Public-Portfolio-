1:40 / Silent / 24 fps

FREND orbital replacement unit exchange

A FREND arm removes the failed Cargo Buddy module, carries it above the bus rim and seats it in the bay. It then picks a replacement, inserts it into the customer opening and retreats. Red identifies the failed unit, green the replacement, cyan the empty slot and purple the quarantine location. Transparent hull views expose the recessed interfaces. The CAD arms retain their native dimensions; bay supports and customer receiving interfaces are proposed additions. Motion timing is cinematic, with loads and attitude response outside this animation.
