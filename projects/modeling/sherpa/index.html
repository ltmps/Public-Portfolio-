The Cal Poly Pomona team's NASA RASC-AL concept proposes a servicing platform near Earth-Moon L1. The operations sequence connects deployment, customer capture, robotic servicing and resupply.

Connect capture, tools and fluid transfer.

Concept animations follow customer berthing and robotic tool work. The cutaway connects a tank and hose interface with particle tracers in an assumed lumped tank-state model. These schematic scenes describe the proposed operating sequence and interfaces.

Compare ways to reach and resupply the platform.

The trajectory studies separate an Earth-Moon three-body manifold candidate from direct and return cases modeled as Earth-centered two-body conics. The latter connect to a representative halo state through an idealized position and velocity transition.

These views compare model geometry and coast behavior. The return case ends at an atmospheric-interface boundary; the three-body candidate illustrates one stable-manifold branch.
