SHERPA layout

The intended SHERPA arrangement uses FREND arms on rail carts. A camera can be positioned by any available FREND arm, including an assisting arm observing hose handling. The hose carriages are intended to stow below the rail. The CAD servicing views and hose retrieval and stow sequence provide the mission context.

The training setup uses a fixed external RGB-D camera, a pregrasped connector and a lower purge socket. The next configuration adds a FREND-positioned camera and hose retrieval from the below-rail stow location.

Circular rail: carriage, arm and reel

The SHERPA source rail has a 1.25 m median radius. The model uses a ±0.35 rad arc (0.875 m travel). Carriage yaw follows the track angle; the arm and reel turn with it. The scripted sweep keeps the valve closed and the connector clear of both sockets.

Circular rail: learned near-socket connection

A PPO policy commands seven arm joints, a valve request and the cart drive from 61 instrument observations. This near-socket curriculum starts 40 mm from the selected socket with cart offsets within ±3 mm. Training focuses on final approach and docking.

What the policy learns

PPO learns arm-joint, valve and cart commands. Training begins with a grasped hose near a socket, then progresses to coordinated travel between connection and stow. Reference demonstrations initialize the controller.

Reward favors alignment, seal coverage, adequate preload and stable pressure. Leaks, excess motion and blocked valve requests incur penalties. Success requires at least six of eight loaded seal sectors, sufficient normal force and friction margin, pressure above 85% nominal and leakage below the study limit for a 0.5 s connection hold or 1 s stow hold.

Learning and physics diagnostics

Study settings

The helium model uses a 20 kPa supply differential to vacuum and a closed 5 kPa purge-return reservoir. Pressure affects hose stiffness and connector load; a compressible-orifice model estimates leakage. Study limits are 10−8 kg/s leakage, 60 mm minimum bend radius and 90° hose twist. These are simulation settings for the fixed-deck model.

The Franka geometry comes from NVIDIA’s Isaac Sim asset library. NVIDIA’s SimReady hose reference is licensed CC BY 4.0; the dynamic hose uses a segmented model.
