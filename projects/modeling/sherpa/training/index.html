Arm, hose and rail

Learned helium docking and a linear-rail baseline for the circular-rail trade study.

The local MuJoCo model uses a seven-joint Franka arm, a segmented flexible hose and zero gravity. The spacecraft deck is fixed; spacecraft recoil and attitude motion are outside this model. The rail adds one prismatic degree of freedom, with a provisional 1 m travel range. Both the arm and hose reel ride on the cart; the sockets stay fixed.

Linear versus circular rail

The intended SHERPA configuration is a circular rail. The straight cart shown here is retained as a linear-rail trade-study baseline. It verifies a moving arm/reel assembly, bounded drive force and return to center. Circular-track dynamics and coordinated arm/cart PPO have not yet been simulated.

What the policy learns

The completed fixed-base policy trained for 50,176 environment steps after initialization from four successful reference trajectories. It commands seven joint increments and a valve request, using 54 observations from simulated RGB-D localization, encoders, reel and pressure/contact instruments. Each episode begins pregrasped, 40 mm from its selected socket. Moving between sockets, picking up the hose and coordinated rail control are later stages.

Reward favors alignment, seal contact coverage, adequate preload and a stable pressure hold. Leaks, unnecessary motion and blocked valve requests incur penalties. Success earns +20; leak failure, overload or invalid grasp incurs -20. Success requires at least six of eight loaded seal sectors, adequate normal force and friction margin, pressure above 85% nominal and leakage below the provisional threshold throughout a 0.5 s connection hold or 1 s stow hold.

In two held-out episodes per task, connection succeeded in 2/2 and purge stow in 1/2, with no leak-failure terminations. This small diagnostic sample is not a reliability estimate. The displayed learned videos use a separate evaluation seed. The rail preview is scripted and has not undergone PPO training.

Learning and physics diagnostics

Model assumptions

Helium starts at a provisional 20 kPa differential to external vacuum. A closed 5 kPa return reservoir receives purge flow. Pressure changes empirical hose stiffness and applies a separating load; effective seal area and a compressible-orifice model approximate leakage and recoil. The provisional leak limit is 10-8 kg/s, with a 0.10 s failure dwell. Seal wear, molecular-flow leakage, fluid temperature coupling and real detector calibration are not represented. A liquid capillary trap is deferred.

The Franka geometry originates from NVIDIA's Isaac Sim asset library. The SimReady hose reference is by NVIDIA under CC BY 4.0; the moving hose is an authored segmented dynamics surrogate. Arm and rail geometry here are training substitutes for SHERPA hardware. These simulations do not establish flight qualification.
