Arm motion and body-sweep envelopes

The same three chapters add dashed enclosures around the demonstrated arm-body sweep, with a 100 mm margin. These visual envelopes help compare the motions around the spacecraft. They describe the displayed configurations; reach certification, collision contact and load or attitude qualification require separate analysis.

Client-region inspection poses

Three held FREND endpoint poses show the negative Z solar-root region and the negative X body region through lower and bay cutaways. Each pose uses an assumed inspection standoff. The sequence switches between six camera-and-pose states; coverage is limited to these endpoints and excludes joint travel between them.
